1. **Closed (High): validate the join at engine append.** `run_backtest` calls the per-record form immediately after `record_order!`, inside the order loop, and has no trailing whole-ledger audit (`src/backtest/engine.jl:252-264`). Its inclusive leg interval, `first_leg_id:first_leg_id+n-1`, maps to observation index `order_leg_id-first_leg_id+1`. Thus it neither misses one of the record's fills nor selects a different well-formed order's fill.
